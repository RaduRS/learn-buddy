Learn Buddy car logo quiz

The logos identify the car brands featured in this quiz. Each logo and trademark belongs to its respective owner. Learn Buddy is independent and has no affiliation with the featured brands.

The selected logos come from the VehicleSpecs brand logo collection and the Cardog icon collection. The exact upstream file and revision for each logo are listed in SOURCES.tsv. The CUPRA, Dacia, Mazda and SEAT files show the emblem portion of the source artwork. The source collection licences are included below. They do not grant trademark rights in the logos.
